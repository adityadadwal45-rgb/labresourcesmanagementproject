GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

When is my equipment actually reserved?+

A request holds its time slot while it is pending. The booking is confirmed after an administrator approves it.

What if an instrument is under maintenance?+

Maintenance blocks appear as unavailable slots, so you can choose another time before submitting a request.

Is this connected to my university account?+

This is a front-end demonstration. The sample accounts are stored in the browser app and are not secure authentication.
